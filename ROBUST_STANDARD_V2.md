# Cochrane-Level Meta-Analysis (LLM-Team Standard v2, "Robust")

## Purpose

Build systematic reviews and meta-analyses for Raphael Malikian to Cochrane Handbook v6.5 standard, carried out by an LLM team in place of a human research team, with Raphael as accountable human author. This v2 standard was set by Raphael on 22 October 2026 after the Review 2 audit: close the three step-downs found in Review 2, and spend the extra time, agents and compute needed to do it. His words in substance: make it more robust, implement these steps for every future Cochrane-level review, even if it takes longer and uses more agents and more compute.

Reviews under this standard are Cochrane-level, not Cochrane reviews. They are not submitted to Cochrane (Cochrane requires at least two authors). They are published free only: GitHub repo, Zenodo DOI, medRxiv where the framing fits, one no-fee journal, conference abstracts. One review at a time.

## The non-negotiables (v2 changes in bold)

1. Protocol first. Written, dated protocol with PICO or its topic equivalent, eligibility rules, outcomes with definitions fixed in advance, subgroups and sensitivity analyses pre-specified, and a PROSPERO-ready form prepared before screening. No PROSPERO number is ever backdated or implied; if none was issued before analysis, the manuscript says so plainly.
2. Search, full retrieval. Every accessible source is retrieved in FULL. **A test page, a capped retrieval, or a relevance-ranked first-N sample never counts as a searched source; it is logged as partial, and the review does not proceed to screening on a partial set without the author's explicit decision, recorded in the log.** Blocked sources are retried on at least two separate occasions (and by a second route or free API key where one exists) before being logged as blocked. Licensed databases with no access (Embase, Scopus, Web of Science, CINAHL, CENTRAL) are listed as not run, with ready strings, never implied as searched. Citation chasing is done systematically on all included studies and all competing reviews, and its yield is counted in the PRISMA flow. Every run logs string as run, date, hit count, retrieved count.
3. Dual independent screening. Two screener passes with different written rule sets, run apart, over every deduplicated record. Agreement and Cohen kappa are computed by code and reported with their explanation.
4. **Real adjudication, never a coded rule as the final word.** A coded rule may triage, prioritise, or pass records forward for safety, but it may not finally exclude a record a screener included, and it may not settle a disagreement. Every disagreement (include against exclude, and every include-or-maybe record the triage would drop) is read again by an adjudicator agent that sees the full title and abstract, both screeners' reasons, and the eligibility rules, and records its own call with a reason. Records the adjudicator cannot settle are flagged for the human author by ID. This replaces the Review 2 coded adjudication rule and the Review 2 "take the more cautious judgement" risk-of-bias rule.
5. **Two fully independent extractions.** Two extractor passes, run apart from each other, each extracting every included study in full from its full text onto the piloted form. Neither pass sees the other. A merge step compares the two sheets field by field by code and reports the discrepancy count and rate per field. Every discrepancy is settled by reading the source again (a third extraction reading for that field), not by picking a pass. A verification pass on top of that (hand-checked totals, named arms checked against papers) still happens, but it is an addition, not a substitute. This replaces the Review 2 pattern of one extraction plus a verification pass.
6. Risk of bias in duplicate, adjudicated by reading. Two assessor passes apart, per study and per outcome where the data allow, using RoB 2 / ROBINS-I / QUADAS-2 where they fit, or an adapted tool specified before extraction and named as adapted where they do not. **Disagreements are settled by an adjudicator reading the paper and both rationales — never by a mechanical rule such as taking the more cautious judgement.**
7. Statistics, checked two ways. Random-effects meta-analysis with the model, transformation and estimator named in the protocol, tau squared, I squared and prediction intervals reported; Hartung-Knapp adjustment used or its omission justified in the methods. Poolability is judged per outcome against the fixed definitions; studies that do not map are narrative, never forced. Subgroup claims rest on interaction tests. The statistician hand-checks at least one pool in full and the crude totals of every pool against the extraction sheet. Analysis code and datasets are published so any reader can rebuild every figure.
8. GRADE per critical outcome, with the summary-of-findings table placed before Background, downgrade reasons per outcome stated, and expected pressure points named in the protocol before results are known.
9. Manuscript in Cochrane order, PRISMA 2020 and PRISMA-S, structured abstract under 1,000 words, plain language summary, discussion claims each in the same paragraph as their number, conclusions split into practice and research, no clinical or medication recommendation, declarations with funding, conflicts (none, fully independent), and a factual AI-assistance note. De-AI copy rules apply in full with the audit pass.
10. **Figures built and inspected, not assumed.** Every forest plot draws the pooled estimate as a summary DIAMOND (centre = pooled value, tips = 95% CI) with the prediction interval shown where computed. Plots are sized to fit the printed page before the PDF is built: no figure may exceed the page's usable height, arm-level plots are split into panels, and by-study plots are the manuscript's main figures. **After the PDF and DOCX are built, every figure page is rendered and visually inspected (diamond visible, axis labels present, nothing clipped, no heading orphaned on a blank page, every planned figure actually present) and the inspection is logged. A build that fails inspection does not ship.** (Lesson of Review 2 v1, 2026-10-22: no diamond was drawn, the tall plot overflowed the page, and one plot was never appended.)
11. Disclosure of the team itself. The LLM team log reports, as results: roles and prompts version, search counts, screener agreement and kappa, disagreement counts and types, adjudication outcomes, extraction discrepancy rate per field, risk-of-bias disagreement counts, and every correction made after first build. RAISE 2025 disclosure wording goes in the manuscript.
12. Honesty rules, unchanged. No invented number, count, date or citation; a source not run is never reported as searched; a paywalled text is logged as not retrieved with no workarounds; blocked or partial sources are reported with their limits; discrepancies and incidents are kept visible in the log, not silently resolved.

## Team shape (spend the agents)

Information specialist; Screeners A and B; Adjudicator (screening); Extractors A and B plus a settlement reader; risk-of-bias Assessor pair plus Adjudicator (RoB); Statistician; GRADE assessor; Methods editor; a separate Visual QA pass over the built PDF. Parallel extraction and screening batches are expected. The author has pre-authorised the extra time, agents and compute this takes (2026-10-22); do not shortcut a step to save either.

## Retrofitting an existing review

When the author asks to bring a finished review up to this standard, run the missing steps as a dated robustness pass, log it as an amendment with its own section in the team log, correct the extraction data if the second pass finds errors, re-run the analysis if any pooled input changed, and state in the updated manuscript what changed and whether any pooled number moved. Issue corrected versions on every platform that carries the paper (GitHub commit, Zenodo new version, medRxiv new version once the prior version is posted, journal notified if already submitted). Never silently overwrite the earlier record.
