# Extraction Form (pilot on 2 studies first)

Per study, per arm: study ID | design | setting | population | model + version | generation settings | denominator (exact) | numerator per outcome (exact) | outcome definition as printed (quote) | comparator data | follow-up | funding | notes / verbatim quotes for graded outcomes.

Rules: extract from full text only; record 'not reported' rather than inferring; two independent passes (A and B) in isolated sessions; merge field by field by code; settle every discrepancy by a third reading.
